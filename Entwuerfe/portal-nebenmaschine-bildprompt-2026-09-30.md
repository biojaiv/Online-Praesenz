## Haupt-Prompt (Konzeptblatt, 3 Zustände)

Concept design sheet for a 3D model, clean technical presentation on a very dark
navy background (#0e1622), three panels side by side from left to right, same
object in every panel, same front-facing orthographic camera, labelled
"1 CLOSED", "2 UNFOLDING", "3 OPEN PORTAL".

The object is a flat, disc-shaped mechanical orrery / armillary machine, about
as wide as it is tall, seen face-on: four thin concentric polished titanium
rings (satin blue-grey steel), one outer ring carrying a fine ladder-like
brass rail, a central brass gear core with two nested toothed brass rings,
two tilted elliptical steel orbit rings crossing the centre, several small
gimbal spheres (miniature armillary spheres) and two small brass gears
sitting on the rings, tiny brass bead markers along the orbits. Materials:
brushed titanium, aged brass, dark graphite. Elegant, precise, sparse,
thin-wire construction, no bulk, no ornament, no text on the object.

Panel 1 CLOSED: the machine exactly as described, compact and circular.

Panel 2 UNFOLDING: the central gear core splits into four quarter segments
that slide outward along diagonal tracks; the tilted orbit rings rotate flat
into the frame plane; the four concentric rings separate into four arc
segments each that move outward towards the four corners; thin straight
telescoping steel rails extend between the arcs.

Panel 3 OPEN PORTAL: the machine has become a wide landscape frame with a
16:10 aspect ratio and generously rounded corners. The frame is slim (about
6 % of the frame width) so the empty centre dominates. The four corners are
formed by the former ring arcs, stacked as three to four parallel curved
steel and brass bands; each corner holds one of the former gear-core
quarters and one small gimbal sphere, clearly recognisable as parts of the
original orrery. The straight sides are thin double steel rails with the
brass ladder rail running along them and small brass bead markers. The
inner edge of the frame glows with a soft cold light (#7fd8ff) that fades
inward; the centre aperture is completely empty, flat, evenly dark, clearly
meant to hold a screen or web page later.

Style: high-end product visualisation, physically based rendering, soft
rim light from upper left, subtle reflections, no motion blur, no depth of
field, no background scenery, no people, no text except the three panel
labels. Crisp silhouettes suitable as a modelling reference for Blender.
