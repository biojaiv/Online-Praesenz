Derived maps: scans were recoloured in linear light, roughness ranges bounded,
the slate scan cropped to whole courses, the ceiling planks rotated by 90°.
The sky was rotated so its sun matches the baked sun; the sun was removed for
baking and replaced by a lamp of equal irradiance. Tree foliage was thinned per
leaf card; models were reduced for the web. Porcelain floor and hedge foliage
textures are generated procedurally. All parameters, sources and SHA-256 sums:
Modelle/Frammelsberger_D168/Texturen/manifest-v3.json, v3_sky.json,
manifest-v2.json and garden-manifest.json (local project files).
