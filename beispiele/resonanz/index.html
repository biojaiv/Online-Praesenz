INSTRUMENT · SCULPTURE · SCORE

A sound you can strike

RESONANZ is an instrument and a sculpture at once. Each of the 82 fins is tuned to a step of a pentatonic scale. Tap to strike one, or sweep the mouse quickly across the fins to play them like a harp. Sound starts only when you choose.

  1. StrikeA tap sends a wave through the fins, along the trace and through the lettering.
  2. ShapeSideways changes pitch and form; vertical dragging changes brightness.
  3. RoomDry, studio or cathedral change the reverb and the reflections together.
